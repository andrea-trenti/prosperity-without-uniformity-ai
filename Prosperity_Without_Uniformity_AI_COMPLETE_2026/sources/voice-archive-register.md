## Original status and editing decisions

**Scope:** The goal is conceptual completeness, **not transcript restoration**. The paper does not contain verbatim 45-minute audio transcripts. To publish a verbatim archive, the original audio files or a valid full transcription must be supplied or recovered through an authorized access path.

**Do not silently do any of the following:**

1. Call the author definitively anti- or pro-immigration when the recordings contain both concerns and constructive integration proposals.
2. Reinterpret the 5–10% remarks as a specific annual inflow, foreign-national stock, ancestry measure, or statutory cap. The denominator is not stated.
3. Erase descriptions of aesthetic, including phenotypic, preferences; equally, do not treat them as established biology or civic eligibility criteria.
4. Equate modern labour migration with colonial conquest merely because the author uses the latter as a cautionary analogy.
5. Turn a personal episode about police conduct into verified system-wide evidence.
6. Present hypothesised tariffs, regional migration caps, digital votes or military patrols as policies already shown to improve welfare.
7. Claim the listed recording V09 has a readable transcript.
8. Import the separate earlier discussion on globalisation, biodiversity and happiness as though the speaker had said all its statistical findings in these recordings.
9. Claim the user spoke specific AI propositions before the separate AI text arrives.

## Research integrity statement

The voice recording index is a **source of the author's expressed ideas**, not evidence that their factual propositions are true. A later, separate empirical paper could validate or falsify historical, genetic, institutional and economic claims; this repository deliberately does not conflate that work with the recovery of what the user actually said.
